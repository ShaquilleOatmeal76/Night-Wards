# Night Wards

A browser tower defense game where you defend your settlement from creatures and cryptids that come out at night. Your towers can only hit what your light reaches, so spend your days gathering fuel and building defenses, then keep the darkness pushed back until morning.

## Play

[Play Night Wards](https://shaquilleoatmeal76.github.io/Night-Wards/)
